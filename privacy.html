Privacy Policy

Marvel Magic, by Marvel Corp. Last updated November 21, 2026.

Marvel Magic is a TV guide. It shows what is on across the streaming services and channels you have, then opens the title in that service's own app. It does not stream video. This page explains what the app keeps and where it goes.

What stays on your device

The services you tick, your lists, ratings, watch history, favorite teams, Kids Lock setting and other choices are saved in the app's storage on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it removes them.

If you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional. If you do, Google tells us your name, email address and an account ID (through Google Firebase Authentication). Your settings and lists are then also saved in our Google Firebase database so they follow you to your other TVs and phones, and so you can set up a household (up to 6 people) and approve a TV with a code. If you choose to share a Top list, that list, your service ratings and your first name can be seen by other Marvel Magic users; you can stop sharing at any time.

Membership

Marvel Magic is free for 30 days, then a paid membership sold through Whop. Whop handles payment; we never see your card. When you unlock a device, the email you joined with is sent to our membership check, which asks Whop whether it has an active membership and emails you a 6-digit code to confirm it is you. We keep a list of up to 6 devices per membership.

Guide data and pictures

To show listings, scores and posters, the app loads public information from services such as TVmaze, ESPN and image hosts. Like any web request, these services can see your device's internet address. We do not send them your name, email or lists.

What we do not do

  • No advertising networks and no ad tracking.
  • No analytics or tracking tools.
  • We never sell or rent your information.

Fire TV app

The Fire TV app checks which streaming apps are installed so the Watch button can open the right one. If you turn on the optional "Come back to Marvel Magic" setting (Accessibility), the app only notices which app is on screen, so pressing Back in another app returns you to Marvel Magic. It cannot read what is on the screen or what you type.

Children

Marvel Magic is made for households, with a Kids Lock that hides grown-up titles. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Deleting your information

Sign out and clear the app's data to remove what is on a device. To delete what is saved with your Google sign-in or your membership devices, email us and we will remove it.
